Real Tensor

Curated real-world tensor categories.

TensorSuite collects real-world tensors in supported formats with a focus on sparse tensors exhibiting element-wise and block-wise patterns. The current release includes tensors from eleven categories, with more to be added in the future. Each tensor is redistributed under the license of its upstream dataset, listed on its category page.

Quantum Physics

Physics simulation tensors and structured operator data.

Knowledge Graph

N-ary relational knowledge bases and large subject–relation–object triple stores.

HyperGraph

Hypergraph interactions represented as high-order tensors.

Mobility

Ride pickups, road traffic speeds, and scheduled flights.

Healthcare

ICU time series from PhysioNet and synthetic Medicare claims.

Sensors

Gas sensor arrays and wearable IMU streams.

Recommender

User–video feedback from short-video recommendation logs.

Retail

Customer purchase quantities from online retail transactions.

Social Q&A

User–question–tag interactions on Stack Exchange sites.

Events

Daily actor–actor event counts from GDELT.

Network Traffic

NetFlow event counts from an enterprise network.
