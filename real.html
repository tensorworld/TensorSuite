Real Tensor

Curated real-world tensor categories.

TensorSuite collects real-world tensors in supported formats with a focus on sparse tensors exhibiting element-wise and block-wise patterns. The initial release includes tensors from three categories, with more to be added in the future.

Quantum Physics

Physics simulation tensors and structured operator data.

Knowledge Graph

N-ary relational knowledge base tensors where facts involve more than two entities.

HyperGraph

Hypergraph interactions represented as high-order tensors.
