Synthetic Tensor Generation

Block-wise

Block-wise synthetic generators produce matched pairs of block-sparse tensors for contraction operations. Users can drive generation from an einsum string or from raw mode counts, and configure block partitions, partition-size distributions (random, uniform, mixed, or power-law), and the block-level sparsity pattern. All runs are reproducible, with parameters and the einsum specification recorded in each output bundle.
