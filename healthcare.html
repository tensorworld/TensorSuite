Clinical Time Series and Claims

This TensorSuite collection includes element-wise tensors from openly accessible clinical data: ICU vital signs and lab measurements from PhysioNet challenges and open demo databases, and synthetic Medicare claims from CMS. All sources are open access; none requires credentialing.

License

Each tensor is redistributed under the license of its upstream dataset, listed in the table above and in the bundle README.md. Tensors under a ShareAlike license (CC BY-SA or ODbL) are adaptations of their source data and must be shared under the same license.

DE-SynPUF is synthetic data: the derived tensor is not an official CMS release and is not suitable for clinical or epidemiological inference.
