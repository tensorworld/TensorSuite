Real Tensor

HyperGraph

This TensorSuite collection includes tensors from hypergraph workloads, where interactions involve more than two nodes simultaneously. Each dataset is represented across multiple tensor orders, from 2D up to higher dimensions depending on the dataset.

coauth-DBLP

A temporal higher-order network built from DBLP publications, where nodes are authors and each simplex represents a co-authored publication. Timestamps correspond to the year of publication. Simplices are restricted to at most 25 nodes [1].
