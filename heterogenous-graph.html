Higher-Arity Relational Knowledge Graphs

This TensorSuite collection includes element-wise tensors from higher-arity relational knowledge graph workloads, where relational facts involve more than two entities — also known as n-ary relational knowledge bases, multi-fold relational data, and hyper-relational knowledge graphs.

The tensors are sourced from two widely-used n-ary relational datasets: JF17K [1], derived from the popular knowledge graph Freebase, and WikiPeople [2]. Each dataset is represented at two arity levels, 3-ary and 4-ary, yielding four tensors in total.
